Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? +

We keep this flexible, especially for first-time clients. Small pilot batches are welcome so you can test the branding before committing to a larger recurring order — message us with your expected volume and we'll confirm what works.

How long does delivery take? +

From confirmed design to delivered cartons, the full turnaround is typically 3–5 business days within Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Is your water PSQCA certified? +

Yes. Our water is sourced and filled by Aabetahura Mineral Water, a PSQCA & PSQC certified bottler. Test reports from NIH and Islamabad Diagnostic Center are available on request.

Can I supply my own logo or design? +

Yes — send us your logo and brand colours and we'll design a label mockup for your approval at no extra cost, or you can supply print-ready artwork directly.

Do you offer bulk pricing for large orders? +

Yes. Standard pricing is Rs 40 per 500ml bottle and Rs 75 per 1.5L bottle — larger recurring orders can be discussed for better rates. Contact us with your expected monthly volume.

Which areas do you currently deliver to? +

We currently deliver across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If you're just outside this area, message us — we may still be able to arrange delivery.

What bottle sizes are available? +

500ml and 1.5L are our standard sizes. If you need a different size for your business, let us know your target volume and we'll check feasibility with our supply partner.

Can I order a sample before a bulk order? +

Yes — we recommend a small sample or pilot run first so you can see the label quality and bottle finish in person before placing a larger order.
