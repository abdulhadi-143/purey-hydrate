How it works

From your logo to a finished product, in five steps.

We manage the design and printing directly, then coordinate production and delivery with certified partners — 3 to 5 business days for water bottles, start to finish. Tissue boxes and cards follow the same flow with their own confirmed timelines.

01

Brief

Share your logo, colours, and preferred bottle size. One short call or WhatsApp message.

02

Label Design

We design and print a waterproof label mockup on your exact bottle shape for approval.

03

Produce

Printed labels or packaging go to our certified production partner for filling, sealing, or manufacturing.

04

Quality Check

Every batch is checked for print finish, seal quality, and (for bottles) water safety before packing.

05

Delivered

Sealed cartons delivered directly to your door in Islamabad or Rawalpindi.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? +

200 bottles per size for water bottles — lower than most competitors in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, so you can run a small pilot batch before committing to a larger recurring order. Tissue boxes start at 50 per design, and cards at 1,000.

How long does delivery take? +

From confirmed design to delivered cartons, the full turnaround is typically 3–5 business days within Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Is your water PSQCA certified? +

Yes. Our water is sourced and filled by Aabetahura Mineral Water, a PSQCA & PSQC certified bottler. Test reports from NIH and Islamabad Diagnostic Center are available on request.

Can I supply my own logo or design? +

Yes — send us your logo and brand colours and we'll design a mockup for your approval at no extra cost, or you can supply print-ready artwork directly.

Do you offer bulk pricing for large orders? +

Yes — pricing on all three products drops as your order size increases. See the full tier breakdown in the Pricing section above, or message us with your expected monthly volume for a custom quote.

Which areas do you currently deliver to? +

We currently deliver across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If you're just outside this area, message us — we may still be able to arrange delivery.

What bottle sizes are available? +

500ml and 1.5L are our standard sizes. If you need a different size for your business, let us know your target volume and we'll check feasibility with our supply partner.

What's the minimum order for tissue boxes? +

50 boxes per design. Since tissue boxes are consumable, most clients treat this as an ongoing supply and reorder once they run low — your design stays on file for repeat orders.

Can I get custom loyalty or discount cards? +

Yes — 3.5" x 2" custom cards for loyalty programmes, discounts, or business cards, starting at 1,000 cards per order.

Can I order a sample before a bulk order? +

Yes — we recommend a small sample or pilot run first so you can see the print quality and finish in person before placing a larger order.
